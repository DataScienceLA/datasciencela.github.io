On May 14th at the Venice Arts Center, the Los Angeles Python and Data Science communities were fortunate to hear a talk from world-renowned expert and CEO of Dato, Carlos Guestrin. Often times when you have a meetup where a presenter is tightly identified with a company, the concern is that the presentation will fundamentally become a product pitch. We had nothing to be worried about in this case - Carlos presented for nearly an hour on a wide range of subjects from how the Machine Learning pipeline has changed over time to Deep Learning!

We were incredibly fortunate to have DataScience.LA community member Carl Mullins record the meetup, as well as heroically recover it in the face of data loss. (Thanks, Carl!)

Enjoy watching Carlos’ presentation, and we’re looking forward to seeing you at our next meetup!
