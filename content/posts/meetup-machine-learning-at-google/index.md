### **Machine Learning at Google**

Come and hear about a range of the work that is being done in Google’s Los Angeles office in the area of machine learning:

Scot Fang, Google Engineer\
Scot works on classification of mobile apps into taxonomies of categories. He will discuss aspects of his work related to machine learning, software engineering, and product applications.

Payam Refael, Tech Lead, Google\
Payam works in the signals group at Google LA. The Signals Group builds systems that input text (web page, keyword, creative, query) and output signals for various applications. He will give an overview of these signals and their applications.

We will also host a brief panel discussion with Q&A to follow.
