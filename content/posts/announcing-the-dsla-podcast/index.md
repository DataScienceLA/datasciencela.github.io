You’ve asked and we’ve listened. The audio content from our DataScience.LA interviews will now be released as a podcast! Check us out on [iTunes](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/datascience.la-podcast/id909462080?mt=2) or point your podcast app to [here](http://dsla.podomatic.com/rss2.xml)!

We’ve already released the audio from the userR! conference interviews of [Hadley Wickham](/a-conversation-with-hadley-wickham-the-user-2014-interview/), [Max Kuhn](/a-conversation-with-max-kuhn-the-user-2014-interview/), and [Tal Galili](/a-conversation-with-tal-galili-at-user-2014/) on iTunes and hope to release upcoming interviews in podcast form shortly after our video blog posts. Thanks for being a part of the DataScience.LA community and we look forward to hearing more from you!

A huge thank you to DataScience.LA contributor [Jeff Weakley](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeffweakley) who recorded such fantastic audio in our interviews that it made the conversion to podcast possible!
