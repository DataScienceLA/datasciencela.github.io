Our next Data Science meetup:

**Winning Data Science Competitions**

**by Jeong-Yoon Lee**

In this talk, I am going to share my experiences in data science competitions. After an overview of the competition structure and best practices, I will present the 1st prize winner’s solution for KDD Cup 2015 with the behind-the-scenes story.

Speaker: Jeong-Yoon Lee is a Chief Data Scientist at Conversion Logic. Jeong is one of top competitors at Kaggle (ranked 14th out of 370K), and has won many data science competitions including KDD Cup 2012 and 2015. Prior to joining Conversion Logic, Jeong worked at Demand Media and Opera Solutions focusing on machine learning and data analytics. He received his PhD in computer science and MS in electrical engineering at USC.
