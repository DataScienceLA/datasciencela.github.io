We are a team of volunteers dedicated to building a world-class data science community in Los Angeles for the benefit of all.

## Leadership:

#### Szilard Pafka {#szilard}
{{< avatar src="head1b.jpg" >}}

Szilard is an [expert data scientist](http://www.linkedin.com/in/szilard) and a leader of the Los Angeles data community. He brings extensive theoretical knowledge (PhD) along with more than 15 years of practical experience in advanced data analysis and developing analytical systems. He is currently the Chief Scientist at Epoch and the founder/organizer/co-organizer of several popular data science meetups in LA ([R](http://www.meetup.com/LA-RUG), [DataVis](http://www.meetup.com/datavis-la/), [Data Science/Machine Learning](http://www.meetup.com/LA-Machine-Learning/), [Big Data](http://www.meetup.com/Los-Angeles-Big-Data-Users-Group/)). Follow him on Twitter [@DataScienceLA](https://twitter.com/DataScienceLA). **Update: Szilard moved from California to Texas in July 2021 and is no longer involved with any activities in LA or California.**

#### Amelia McNamara {#amelia}
{{< avatar src="Amelia_Original_1FINAL2_150x150.jpg" >}}

Amelia is a statistics PhD candidate at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). Her research interests include statistical computing, data visualization, spatial statistics, and statistics education. She is currently working with teachers in the Los Angeles Unified School District to bring "data science" into high school classrooms as part of the [Mobilize project](http://www.mobilizingcs.org). You can follow her on Twitter [@AmeliaMN](https://twitter.com/AmeliaMN), or read her [personal blog](http://www.stat.ucla.edu/~amelia.mcnamara/blog).

#### Subash D’Souza {#subash}
{{< avatar src="subash-team.png" >}}

Subash is a leader of the Big Data community in Los Angeles. He speaks regularly at conferences, has been recognized by Cloudera as a Champion of Big Data, and envisions himself as a [Big Data Evangelist](http://www.linkedin.com/in/sawjd). He is the founder of Big Data Camp LA, a free community conference, and he runs the LA Big Data, LA HBase, and LA Apache Spark meetups. He is currently the Principal Hadoop Architect at Lynda.com. Follow him on Twitter [@sawjd22](https://twitter.com/sawjd22).
