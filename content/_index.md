title: "Data Science LA"
description: "Archive of the DataScience.LA community blog, 2014–2021"
---

From 2014 to 2021 **DataScience.LA** was the blog of the Los Angeles data science community: summaries and videos of LA R, PyData, DataVis and Machine Learning meetup talks, interviews from the useR! 2014 conference at UCLA, benchmarks, and essays. This is a read-only archive of the original site. Comments are preserved but closed.
